# Pure Heart 1.9.0

Zero-tolerance NSFW filter for Monika After Story. Removes the revealing outfits and the risque topics, keeps hugs, kisses and everything wholesome untouched.

## New in this version

- Topics now live in their own **Twilight** category (Talk > More) instead of Misc.
- New topic: **About the Creator**, where Monika explains why this mod exists.
- Dialogue polish across all topics.

## Carried over from 1.8.0

- Outfit lists update online from [PureHeart-Lists](https://github.com/Twil1ght-L4D2/PureHeart-Lists). New bans and approvals reach every player on their next launch, no reinstall needed.
- Offline fallback: last downloaded lists, then the built-in ones. The filter can never break or empty out.

## Install

1. Download `PureHeart-Submod-1.9.0.zip` below.
2. Extract it into your MAS `game/Submods/` folder.
3. Start the game. Monika thanks you the first time.

Or install in one click with the [MAS Big Loader](https://github.com/Twil1ght-L4D2/MAS-Submod-List).
